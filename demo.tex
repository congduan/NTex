\cmr This is the first paragraph of a richer test document. It is long enough to wrap around into several lines, and it deliberately uses words that exercise the newly implemented features: ligatures like first, office, efficient, and flame (the f-i, f-l and f-f pairs become single glyphs); kerning pairs such as the v in several, the w in two words, and the n in into; and the spacefactor adjustments after commas, semicolons, and periods. All of these subtle details are now typeset exactly as pdfTeX would, down to the last scaled point.
\par
\cmr The second paragraph shows automatic pagination: it continues with more sentences so that the total height exceeds the page target of 300pt set by the vsize parameter. When the vertical list overflows the page builder fires up the current page at the best feasible break, ships it out, and starts a fresh one. The topskip and maxdepth parameters control the first line of each page and the depth of the final line.
\par
\cmr Finally, the third paragraph demonstrates the interplay of parskip, baselineskip, and lineskip between paragraphs and lines. Interline glue keeps a constant baseline distance of twelve points, while parskip adds six points of space with two points of stretch between the paragraphs themselves, giving the document a more relaxed, readable rhythm. This is where the paragraph spacing and line spacing logic meet.
