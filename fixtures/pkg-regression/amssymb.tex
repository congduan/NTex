\section{Amssymb}
The sets $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{N}$, and $\mathbb{Q}$ are standard
number systems drawn from the msbm font.
Relations: $x \lesssim y$, $p \equiv q$, $A \subseteq B$.
Symbols: $\varnothing$, $\nexists$, $\complement$, $\heartsuit$.
